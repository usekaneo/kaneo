## How Kaneo is organized

A **workspace** holds your team, projects, and shared labels. A **project** holds a body of work, such as a website launch. A **task** is one piece of that work, with its own status, assignee, description, and conversation.

If someone invited you, open their invitation link and sign in with the email address they invited. You can join their workspace without creating a new one.
