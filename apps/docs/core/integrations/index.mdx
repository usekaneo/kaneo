Connect the tools your team already uses. Repository connections and shared notifications belong to a project. Personal notification channels belong to your account.

## Repositories

  </Card>
  <Card title="Gitea" icon="git-branch" href="/core/integrations/gitea/setup">

  <Card title="GitLab" icon="gitlab" href="/core/integrations/gitlab/setup">
    Connect GitLab.com or your own instance to sync issues and track merge requests.

GitHub, Gitea, and GitLab have connection forms under **Settings → Projects → your project → Integrations**. Follow the provider guide to connect a repository and configure its webhook.

## Team notifications

Send selected project events to a shared channel. Open **Settings → Projects → your project → Integrations** to connect these services.

  <Card title="Discord" icon="message-circle" href="/core/integrations/discord">

  <Card title="Mattermost" icon="messages-square" href="/core/integrations/mattermost">

## Personal notifications

Receive updates meant for you, such as assignments, mentions, and due-date reminders. Connect these under **Settings → Account → Notifications**, then choose the workspaces and projects that can send them.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="ntfy" icon="bell" href="/core/integrations/ntfy">
    Publish your notifications to an ntfy topic.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Gotify" icon="smartphone" href="/core/integrations/gotify">
    Receive notifications through your Gotify server.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Email" icon="mail" href="/core/functional/account-notifications#2-connect-a-channel">
    Send updates to your account email. Self-hosted instances need SMTP.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Personal webhook" icon="webhook" href="/core/integrations/personal-webhooks">
    Forward your own notifications to an HTTP endpoint.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

[Notification preferences](/core/functional/account-notifications) control the events and workspace rules for all four channels.

## Looking for sign-in or file storage?

Sign-in providers are configured by the instance operator: [GitHub](/core/social-providers/github), [Google](/core/social-providers/google), [Discord](/core/social-providers/discord), or [custom OAuth/OIDC](/core/social-providers/custom-oauth).

For attachments, use [Silo](/core/installation/silo) or [another S3-compatible provider](/core/installation/storage-backends).
