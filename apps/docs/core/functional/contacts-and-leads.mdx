---
title: "Contacts and leads"
description: "Keep a shared list of customers and prospects, link tasks to them as leads, and follow every interaction on a timeline."
---

Contacts is a light, shared address book for sales. A **lead** is not a new kind of object: it is an ordinary task linked to a contact. Because leads are tasks, they already appear on the board and the calendar, and they use due dates and reminders like any other work.

## Add a contact

1. Open **Contacts** in the sidebar.
2. Choose **Add contact**.
3. Enter a name. Optionally add a phone number, an email address and notes.
4. Optionally choose a **type** and a **location**, then save. Locations can run to hundreds of islands, so that picker is searchable: start typing a name. A contact with neither shows an em dash (—), meaning not set.

Everyone in the workspace shares the same list. Search by name, phone or email, and filter by type and location. The **Leads** column counts the tasks linked to each contact that you can open. Types and locations come from lists you control, so filtering stays clean.

## Manage types and locations

Workspace owners and admins edit the lists under **Settings, Workspace, Contacts**. There are two cards, **Contact types** and **Locations**. A new workspace starts with the types Retailer, Distributor, End user, Partner and Other, and the locations Malé, Hulhumalé, North atolls, Central atolls, South atolls and Other.

- **Rename:** click a name, type, and press Enter or click away to save. Press Escape to cancel. Contacts keep their type or location when you rename it, and every contact shows the new name.
- **Reorder:** use the up and down buttons. The order is the one people see in the pickers.
- **Add:** type a name in the add field. To add many at once, paste into **Add several**, one name per line. Blank lines, repeats (ignoring capitals) and names already in the list are skipped, and you see how many were added and skipped. A single paste can hold any number of lines; a list holds up to 2000 entries.
- **Delete:** the confirmation says how many contacts use it. Those contacts keep everything else but become not set. Deleting does not touch tasks. You cannot delete the last entry of a list, so there is always something to choose. To undo a delete, add the name again; contacts that lost it stay not set.

Everyone who can read the workspace can use the lists when adding or editing a contact; only owners and admins (the **manage settings** permission) can change them.

## Turn a task into a lead

Open a task and set **Contact** in its properties. The contact's name then appears on the task's card on the board and on its bar in the calendar. Set the contact to **No contact** to unlink it. Deleting a contact keeps its tasks and only removes the link.

Use the task's **due date** as the next follow-up. Your usual reminders and the calendar then show who needs a call today.

## Follow-up timeline

Select a contact's name to open its panel:

- **Leads:** the contact's tasks, next follow-up first. Overdue follow-ups are red and today's are amber.
- **Log interaction:** write what happened (a call, a visit, a quote), choose the lead if there is more than one, and optionally set the **next follow-up** date. The note is saved as a comment on the lead and the date becomes its due date.
- **Timeline:** every comment on the contact's leads, newest first, with who wrote it and when. Comments from integrations are included.

Because an interaction is an ordinary comment, it also shows in the task itself, and anything you comment on a lead appears on the contact's timeline.

## Who can see and change contacts

| Action | Needed |
| --- | --- |
| See contacts and their timelines | Read access to the workspace |
| Add a contact | Permission to create tasks |
| Edit a contact or log an interaction | Permission to update tasks |
| Delete a contact | Permission to delete tasks |

The timeline and the lead counts leave out leads in projects you cannot open. Contacts are shared across the whole workspace, so everyone who can read the workspace sees every contact's phone number, email and notes.

## Automate it

The API and the MCP server expose contacts, so a chat bot or an AI assistant can add contacts and leads. See the [MCP guide](/core/integrations/mcp) and the **Contacts** endpoints in the API reference. `list_contact_options` returns the type and location keys and labels; `create_contact` and `list_contacts` accept either the key or the label (any capitalisation) and reject unknown values with the valid choices. Contacts come back with `typeLabel` and `regionLabel` next to the keys. The options have their own endpoints for listing, adding (one or many), renaming, reordering and deleting. Create a lead with `create_task` and a `contactId`, link an existing task with `set_task_contact`, and log an interaction with `create_task_comment`.
