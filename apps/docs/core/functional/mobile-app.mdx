Kaneo for iPhone and iPad is on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6828574501). An Android app is coming soon.

The app covers the work you do away from your desk: your boards, task details, My tasks, your inbox, and activity. Descriptions are edited inline with a formatting toolbar and save as you type.

## Sign in with Kaneo Cloud

1. Open the app and choose **Kaneo Cloud**.
2. Sign in with your email and password, or select **Email me a sign-in code**.
3. To use GitHub, Google, Discord, or single sign-on, select **Continue in browser** and approve the device on the Kaneo website.

The app is free for Kaneo Cloud accounts. Your workspace's Cloud plan is managed on the web, as before.

## Connect a self-hosted server

1. Open the app and choose **Self-hosted**.
2. Under **Server address**, enter the address you open Kaneo at, for example `https://kaneo.example.com`. If your API runs on a separate host, enter the API address instead. Use `http://` only for servers without TLS.
3. Select **Connect**, then sign in with the same methods your instance offers on the web.

Connecting to a self-hosted server is a one-time $9.99 purchase that supports Kaneo's development:

- It belongs to you, through your Apple ID, not to the server. One purchase works with every self-hosted server you connect to.
- Each person who wants to use the app with a self-hosted server buys it once for themselves.
- On a new device, select **Restore purchase** to unlock it again with the same Apple ID.

The app is open source, so you can also build it yourself from the [repository](https://github.com/usekaneo/kaneo/tree/main/apps/mobile).

### Sign-in methods on self-hosted servers

- **Continue in browser** uses device authorization. It works with the default configuration. If you set `DEVICE_AUTH_CLIENT_IDS`, include `kaneo-cli` in the list. See [environment variables](/core/installation/environment-variables).
- **Use an API key** works even when other sign-in methods are turned off. Create a key on the web under **Settings → Account → API keys**.
- **Create account** appears only when registration is open on your instance.

## Delete your account

Open **Account** from your avatar, then select **Delete account** under **Danger zone**. Type your email address to confirm. If you signed in a while ago, enter your password, or sign out and back in first.

Deleting your account in the app works the same as on the web. See [what happens to your data](/core/functional/manage-your-account#what-happens-to-your-data).
