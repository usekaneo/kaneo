Open **Settings → Account → Preferences**. These are your preferences, so changing them does not change how your teammates see Kaneo.

## Appearance and layout

Choose your theme, default task view, compact mode, and whether the sidebar starts open or closed. You can also change the language and calendar preferences available on this page.

## Task details in views

Choose whether to show task numbers, assignees, dates, labels, and priority indicators. Hiding a field changes its display, not the data on the task.

Use **Reset** to return preferences to their defaults.

## Team settings

Shared settings live elsewhere:

- [Workspace labels](/core/functional/manage-workspace-labels) organize work for everyone.
- [Project workflows](/core/functional/configure-workflows) define status columns.
- [Members and roles](/core/functional/collaborate-with-team) control access.
- [Notifications](/core/functional/account-notifications) control the updates you receive.
