---
title: My tasks
description: See everything assigned to you across every project and workspace in one place.
---

**My tasks** gathers every open task assigned to you, from all the projects in all the workspaces you belong to, on one page. Open it from the **My tasks** entry at the top of the sidebar, or go to `/dashboard/my-tasks`.

## Views

- **Board** shows your tasks in status columns. Columns with the same slug across projects are merged (projects created from the defaults share *To do*, *In progress*, *In review* and *Done*); a column that exists in only one project appears as its own column. Switch to **List** with the toggle in the toolbar.
- **Calendar** places tasks with a start or due date on a month grid.

Each task shows its own project prefix (`PROJECT-12`), so you can tell projects apart at a glance. Click a task to open its details; the context menu lets you change status, priority, due date, labels or assignee just as on a project board.

## Filters and sorting

The toolbar offers the same filters as a project board: status, priority, due date and labels, plus a **Project** filter. Your filter and sort choices are remembered between visits. Tasks are sorted by due date by default, with undated tasks last.

## What is not shown

- Tasks in the backlog (**planned**) and **archived** tasks. Open the project's backlog to see those.
- Tasks in archived projects.
- Tasks from workspaces you are no longer a member of, even if they still show you as the assignee.

## Good to know

- Drag-and-drop reordering is not available here, because positions belong to each project's own board. Use the context menu or the task details to change status.
- The page updates live when a task is assigned to you, reassigned or edited.
- Every open task assigned to you is loaded, so filters and search cover all of them.
- The actions offered on each task follow your role in that task's workspace, which may differ from the one open in the sidebar.
