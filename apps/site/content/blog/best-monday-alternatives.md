monday.com is a work OS. Colourful boards, dashboards, forms, automation recipes, CRM and HR templates, and a marketplace. For an operations or marketing team that wants all of that in one place, it delivers.

Three things send people looking for an alternative, and they are usually not about features.

The first is seat blocks. monday.com sells users in tiers rather than by exact headcount, so a team of eleven often pays for fifteen. The second is the free plan, which is capped tightly enough that it functions as a demo. The third is single sign-on, which sits on the Enterprise tier.

This is a comparison of the 10 tools worth looking at, including the open-source ones you can host yourself where team size stops being a billing event entirely. Prices were checked in August 2026. Vendors move tiers frequently, so verify before you commit.

## Why teams look for a monday.com alternative

**Seat blocks.** Pricing is sold in user tiers, so adding one person can push you into the next block. Teams that hover near a boundary end up paying for capacity they do not use, and the bill jumps rather than climbs.

**A three-board free plan.** The free tier is limited enough that it does not survive contact with a real team, which turns evaluation into a purchase decision early.

**Enterprise-tier single sign-on.** SSO sits on Enterprise, which is quote-only. That is the industry norm rather than a monday.com quirk, but it is where a lot of teams reassess. We keep a running list of [tools that include SSO for free](/guides/project-management-tools-with-free-sso).

**No self-hosting.** monday.com is cloud only. There is no configuration, tier, or contract that puts the database on your infrastructure, so data residency requirements have to be solved another way.

**More product than you need.** Dashboards, forms, CRM boards, and a marketplace are genuinely useful if you use them. If your team wants a board, a backlog, and assignees, you are paying for a platform to get a tracker.

**Cons:** Cloud only and proprietary. Organised around message boards and to-do lists rather than boards and backlogs. No single sign-on.

## Migrating off monday.com

monday.com exports boards to Excel and CSV and has a documented GraphQL API, so a scripted migration is realistic. What tends to break is column types, especially status columns, mirror columns, and formula columns, which have no equivalent in most trackers.

Export one active board first and import it somewhere as a test. Map status columns to the target tool's workflow columns deliberately rather than by name matching, check that assignees resolve by email, and confirm that update threads and files came across. Keep monday.com readable for a quarter rather than cancelling the same week. Kaneo has a public REST API and per-project JSON import, and we will help with a migration script if you open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/usekaneo/kaneo).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best free alternative to monday.com?

ClickUp's free tier is the most feature-generous of the hosted plans and Trello's is the most immediately usable. If you are willing to run a server, Kaneo is MIT licensed and self-hostable. Once you price in the server, the upgrades, and the backups, a managed plan usually wins, and Kaneo Cloud starts at $4 a month with single sign-on included.

### Is there an open-source alternative to monday.com?

Yes, though nothing open source replicates the full work-OS scope. Kaneo (MIT), Plane (AGPL-3.0 Community edition), OpenProject (GPLv3 Community edition), and Vikunja (AGPLv3) are the main self-hostable options. Each covers the project-tracking part well and none of them ship a CRM.

### Can monday.com be self-hosted?

No. monday.com is cloud only with no on-premise option at any tier. If self-hosting is a hard requirement, it has to be a different tool rather than a different plan.

### Does monday.com have a seat minimum?

Users are sold in tiers rather than by exact headcount, so a team whose size falls between tiers pays for the higher one. This is the most common cost complaint and the reason exact-headcount pricing is worth checking on any alternative you shortlist.

### Which monday.com alternative is cheapest for a large team?

Among managed tools, Basecamp's flat rate wins at genuine scale, and Kaneo Cloud at $5 per user a month with no seat minimum is roughly half of Asana's entry tier and bills on exact headcount. Self-hosting an open-source tool is cheaper on paper, because the bill is a server rather than a headcount, provided you have someone to run it.

### Does monday.com include single sign-on?

SSO is an Enterprise-tier feature. If you need SAML or OIDC against your own identity provider without an Enterprise contract, our [guide to tools with free SSO](/guides/project-management-tools-with-free-sso) covers where it is included.
