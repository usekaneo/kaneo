Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026 · Version 2

Overview

Quills is a hosted project management service operated by Playbook Interactive Media, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (“Playbook Media,” “we,” or “us”). This policy covers the Quills service at quills.playbookmedia.io. It does not describe the separate Shots service or an independently hosted project management instance.

Quills stores account information and workspace content on our servers so you can work with your team across devices. We use that information to provide, secure, and support the service. We do not sell your personal information.

Information we collect

  • Account and sign-in information. Your name, email address, verification status, profile image if provided, and the records needed to authenticate you. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes. Email codes and verification links are time-limited.
  • Sign-in providers. If you choose GitHub, Google, or WorkOS, we receive the account identifiers, basic profile information, email verification information, and authentication tokens needed for that sign-in. We do not receive your password for those providers.
  • Workspace content. Workspace names, settings, memberships, roles, invitations, projects, tasks, descriptions, comments, attachments, activity records, and other information you or your team put into Quills. Invitations include the recipient’s email address.
  • Connected integrations. If you connect an integration, we process the connection information, credentials, events, and content needed for the features you enable. The permissions requested are shown when you connect the service.
  • Technical and security information. IP addresses, browser and device information, session records, request times, errors, and administrative audit records used to operate and protect Quills.
  • Messages to us. If you contact us, we receive your message and the contact information you provide.

What stays in your browser

Your browser keeps session cookies, preferences, and temporary application data to support sign-in and the interface. Workspace data and uploaded attachments are sent to the service; they are not limited to storage on your device. Clearing browser storage does not delete your account or server-side workspace content.

How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, store and display workspace content, enforce permissions, deliver live updates, send account and invitation emails, operate integrations you choose, answer support requests, prevent abuse, investigate faults, and meet applicable legal obligations. Workspace owners control who joins their workspaces and what members can access.

We process workspace content to provide Quills to the customer that controls that workspace. If your request concerns a team’s content, contact the workspace owner or contact us so we can help direct the request. We do not use Google sign-in data for advertising.

Hosting, storage, and service providers

Our current deployment uses these providers for the purposes described below:

  • DigitalOcean. Application hosting in its New York region in the United States.
  • Microsoft Azure. Managed PostgreSQL database storage, database backups, and Redis delivery infrastructure in the North Central US region.
  • Cloudflare. DNS, network delivery and security, and private R2 storage for uploaded files. Cloudflare operates infrastructure in multiple locations.
  • Amazon Web Services (Amazon SES) and Resend. Transactional email, including sign-in codes, verification links, invitations, and password recovery messages. These providers process recipient addresses, message content, and delivery events.
  • WorkOS, GitHub, and Google. Identity and authentication services when you choose those sign-in methods. WorkOS may also connect to an identity provider selected by your organization.

Providers process information as needed to supply their services and under their applicable terms and privacy policies. Information may be processed in the United States and other countries where those providers operate. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the service, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or business transfer subject to appropriate protections.

Google user data

Google sign-in is used to identify and authenticate your account using the identity permissions you grant. Quills’ use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. You can review or revoke access through your Google Account permissions.

Cookies and your choices

Quills uses authentication cookies and browser storage for sessions and preferences. Blocking required cookies can prevent sign-in from working. These public legal pages do not load advertising or analytics scripts. An identity provider may use its own cookies when you visit its sign-in pages, under that provider’s policy.

You can choose a supported sign-in method, manage your account and sessions, and use workspace permissions and integration controls where available. Revoke an integration’s access in that provider’s account settings when you no longer want it to have access.

Retention and deletion

We retain account and workspace information while it is needed to provide the service, and as needed for security, support, dispute resolution, and legal obligations. Workspace owners manage team content. Deleting a personal account does not necessarily remove content or business records that belong to a shared workspace.

The current managed database is configured with seven days of backup retention. Deleted information can remain in backups until they rotate out. Service providers may retain operational records under their own retention settings and applicable obligations. Contact us if you need help with account or workspace deletion.

Security

We use encrypted network connections, access controls, workspace permissions, private file storage, and password hashing to protect information. No service can guarantee absolute security. Keep account credentials and sign-in codes private, and tell us promptly if you suspect unauthorized access.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, object to or restrict processing, or withdraw consent. Contact team@playbookmedia.io to make a request. We may need to verify your identity and your authority over the information before acting. You may also have the right to complain to a relevant data protection authority.

Children

Quills is intended for business and professional use and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their personal information. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information to the service.

Changes and contact

We update this policy when the service or its processing changes. The date and version above identify the current policy. We will provide any notice required by applicable law for material changes.

Privacy questions and requests: team@playbookmedia.io. Our Terms of Service govern use of the hosted service.
